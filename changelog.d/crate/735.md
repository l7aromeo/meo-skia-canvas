### Fixed

- **A gradient mixed in `GradientColorSpace::ProphotoRgb` follows CSS Color 4's curve, linear below 16/512, where it drew dark ramps up to 4 levels off; an SVG export draws it as an image, since SVG's own gradient mixes in sRGB.** ([#753](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/753))
- **On Windows, a DLL linking the crate stays loaded for the life of the process once the crate starts a thread, so a pool thread parked in its code no longer crashes the process after the DLL is unloaded.** ([#735](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/735))
- **A full-page `fill_rect` or `fill` under a transform that shrinks it, and a full-page `clear_rect` under a clip, inside a layer or turned by the transform, no longer erase what they do not reach.** ([#808](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/808))
- **An opaque `draw_image` or a `put_image_data` covering the whole page releases what was drawn before it, as a full-page opaque fill or `clear_rect` does, so a page cleared that way each frame no longer grows; a background drawn in pieces still keeps what it covers, as [docs/performance.md](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/blob/main/docs/performance.md) explains.** ([#741](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/741))
