### Fixed

- **A shadow cast with `Context2D::set_shadow_blur` is the exact Gaussian blur of what casts it, on the CPU and the GPU, where Skia's approximation was up to 11 levels off it.** ([#737](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/737), [#669](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/669))
- **A draw under `Context2D::set_filter_css` casts the shadow of what the filter makes, where it cast the shadow of the draw unfiltered.** ([#737](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/737), [#713](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/713))
- **A draw casting a shadow lands the pixels it lands casting none, where a draw under `blur()` moved by up to 15 levels and one under `drop-shadow()` further.** ([#737](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/737), [#714](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/714))
- **An image or a canvas drawn under `Context2D::set_filter_css` with several colour functions, and text under a `blur()` and them, takes the tint the functions make together, where rounding between them moved an opaque pixel by up to 4 levels.** ([#737](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/737), [#730](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/730))
- **A fill or stroke in a pattern or a gradient under a CSS `blur()` is the exact Gaussian blur of what it draws, where Skia's blur was up to 5.78 levels off it.** ([#737](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/737), [#715](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/715))
- **A rectangle under a CSS `blur()` on a GPU canvas is its exact blur to the byte, as on a CPU canvas, where the GPU was up to 0.86 of a level off it.** ([#737](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/737), [#708](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/708))
