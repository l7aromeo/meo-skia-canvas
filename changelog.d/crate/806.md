- **Translucent text on a CPU canvas lands the exact product of its coverage and its alpha, where about one covered pixel in ten stood a level off it.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#761](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/761))
- **Translucent text on a GPU canvas keeps the glyph contrast of the colour it is drawn in, where a grey at an alpha of 0.3 landed up to 8 levels below the exact product.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#778](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/778))
- **A gradient a GPU canvas draws on the CPU -- a translucent two-point conical one, one interpolated in a space other than the canvas's, one cast with a shadow or drawn through a blend -- lands the same bytes inside `Context2D::save_layer` as on the canvas itself.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#772](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/772))
- **`Context2D::get_image_data` of part of a canvas stores the bytes a read of the whole canvas stores there, for a path's anti-aliased edge, a gradient, a pattern, or an image off whole pixels or scaled.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#771](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/771))
- **On an x86-64 CPU, a canvas drawn in with `Context2D::draw_canvas` under `destination-atop`, `source-in`, `copy` or another blend that changes the page beyond it lands its own pixels at a whole-pixel offset, as `Context2D::draw_image` of them does, where translucent pixels stood a level off.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#822](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/822))
- **A canvas never cleared that canvases keep being drawn into no longer holds every one: past a few pages of them it keeps its own pixels in their place.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#823](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/823))
- **A canvas never cleared, filled each frame with a new `Context2D::create_pattern_from_canvas` pattern of a changing canvas, lets the patterns' pixels go past a few pages, where it kept a page for every pattern, two for a canvas holding a drawn canvas.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#832](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/832))
