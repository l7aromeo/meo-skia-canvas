### Fixed

- **A run of text casting a shadow under a composite operation other than `BlendMode::SourceOver` composites its shadow and then the run as one image, as the HTML Standard paints it.** ([#740](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/740))
- **A shadowed rectangle, path or image under `SourceIn`, `SourceOut`, `DestinationIn`, `DestinationOut`, `DestinationAtop` or `Copy` composites its shadow and then itself, each over the whole canvas.** ([#786](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/786))
- **`Context2D::draw_paragraph` under a global alpha or a composite operation composites the paragraph's backgrounds, each text shadow and its text one after another, as `fill_rect` and `fill_text` do.** ([#788](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/788))
- **A run of text holding colour glyphs casts one shadow from the whole run, as a run of plain glyphs does, not one for each colour glyph and one for the plain glyphs between them.** ([#787](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/787))
- **A canvas drawn into another with `Context2D::draw_canvas` draws its text with the glyph edges its own pixels have, under its own `text_contrast` and `text_gamma`; between canvases made with different ones, it is drawn as an image, in a PDF or SVG export too.** ([#805](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/805))
