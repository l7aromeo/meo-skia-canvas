### Fixed

- **A filtered draw on a CPU canvas that reaches past the canvas's top or left edge draws its curves as the canvas does, rather than up to tens of levels off along them.** ([#507](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/507))
- **A shape or text in one colour blurred by 5 pixels or more on a CPU canvas is rounded once from its exact blur, as a smaller blur is, so an edge's colour, and a translucent run of text's alpha, no longer land a level off it.** ([#660](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/660))
