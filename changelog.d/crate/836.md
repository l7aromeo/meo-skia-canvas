### Fixed

- **`Svg::rasterize` keeps the colours outside sRGB of an image a document embeds, such as a Display P3 PNG's, for a wide canvas it is drawn onto, as Chrome does, where they were clipped to sRGB.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#734](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/734))
- **An SVG document's `href` names what `xlink:href` does -- an `<image>`, a `<use>` target, a gradient or pattern to inherit from -- and wins over an `xlink:href` beside it.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#734](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/734))
- **On Linux, `Context2D::measure_text` and every drawn run advance each glyph by its font units times the size, as macOS and Windows do, rather than about 2/65536 px an em over at 16px in a 1000-unit face, with the patched Skia archives.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#695](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/695))
- **An SVG document's paint in any CSS Color 4 form -- `color()`, `oklch()`, `#rrggbbaa`, space-separated `rgb()`, `transparent` -- is the colour it names, as a `fillStyle` reads it, and a `currentcolor` in lower case is the `color` it inherits, where both drew black.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#815](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/815))
- **An SVG document's translucent `flood-color` keeps its own alpha, multiplied by `flood-opacity` as Filter Effects says, where it drew opaque, with the patched Skia archives.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#815](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/815))
- **An SVG document's `<line>` paints no fill, since a line has no interior, as Chrome draws it, where its fill -- black unless set -- drew as a hairline along it, with the patched Skia archives.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#825](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/825))
- **An SVG document's `<feFlood>` in a linearRGB filter, `color-interpolation-filters`' initial value, floods in its own colour, as Chrome draws it, where `#808080` flooded at 188, with the patched Skia archives.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#826](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/826))
- **An SVG document's `<feDiffuseLighting>` and `<feSpecularLighting>` in a linearRGB filter light in the light's own colour, as Chrome draws them, where `#808080` lit at 188, and keep `lighting-color` in float where Chrome rounds it to a byte, with the patched Skia archives.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#826](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/826))
- **On x86-64, an image drawn scaled, at a fractional offset or as a pattern at a global alpha (`Context2D::set_global_alpha`) below one scales its bytes by the alpha correctly rounded, as on arm64, where up to two levels apart, with the patched Skia archives.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#828](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/828))
- **On x86-64, an antialiased edge, a mask and a clip apply their coverage to a partly covered pixel correctly rounded, as on arm64, where up to two levels apart, with the patched Skia archives.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836))
- **An SVG document's text in a COLR colour font is drawn as `Context2D::fill_text` draws it, where macOS 27.2 drew a COLRv0 face's glyphs as nothing and the platform's scaler drew COLRv1 glyphs in other colours than Chrome's.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#663](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/663))
- **COLR text at full opacity whose glyphs are only fills, drawn with `Context2D::fill_text` or `Context2D::draw_canvas`, exports to SVG as `<path>`s, where it was embedded as pixels.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#663](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/663))
- **A `Shader::two_point_conical_gradient` fill between two circles, or from a point off its end circle's centre, is embedded in an SVG export as pixels, where the export wrote its circles swapped, up to 255 levels off.** ([#836](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/836), [#834](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/834))
