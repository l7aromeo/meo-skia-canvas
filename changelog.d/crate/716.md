### Fixed

- **`fill_text` and `measure_text` place right-to-left text that ends in spaces with the spaces at its start, as Chrome does, when aligned left, centred or to its end.** ([#716](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/716), [#710](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/710))
- **A text decoration runs under the spaces a text ends in, and under text made only of spaces, from `fill_text` and in a `Paragraph`, as Chrome draws it.** ([#716](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/716), [#706](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/706))
- **A `Paragraph`'s background runs behind the spaces each of its lines ends in.** ([#716](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/716), [#706](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/706))
- **`measure_text` of text made only of spaces puts its empty ink box at the start of its advance, where Chrome puts it, rather than at the anchor.** ([#716](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/716), [#711](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/711))
