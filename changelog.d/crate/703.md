### Fixed

- **A canvas holding another drawn into it, drawn with `draw_canvas` into a GPU canvas, keeps its gradients and colour glyphs in the bytes a CPU canvas stores for them.** ([#703](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/703))
- **Glyphs that overlap under `Multiply` through `Luminosity`, on a canvas drawn into a GPU canvas with `Context2D::draw_canvas` or inside a `save_layer` on a GPU canvas, land the bytes a CPU canvas draws.** ([#764](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/764))
- **Text a GPU canvas draws in memory -- a gradient-filled run under a filter, or any text under a filter on a float canvas -- takes `CanvasOptions::text_contrast` and `text_gamma`, not Skia's defaults.** ([#785](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/785))
- **On a GPU canvas a path filled or stroked with a gradient has the anti-aliased edge a CPU canvas gives it, so its shadow and the blur a filter makes of it are those of the edge it draws.** ([#766](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/766))
