### Fixed

- **`Svg::rasterize` and `Image::from_svg_xml` composite a document's translucent draws in sRGB, as a browser does, where they composited in linear light: half-alpha red over blue rasterized as 188, 0, 187 rather than 128, 0, 127.** ([#839](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/839))

### Changed

- **An SVG of a canvas holding a `Context2D::put_image_data`, or a `Context2D::clear_rect` that leaves part of the page, keeps what lies outside the erased region as vectors and text and writes the put as an image of its own, where it embedded everything drawn before it as one image of the page.** ([#827](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/827))
