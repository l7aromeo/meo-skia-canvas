### Fixed

- **A shadow cast with `Context2D::set_shadow_blur` is the exact Gaussian blur of what casts it, on the CPU and the GPU, where Skia's approximation was up to 11 levels off it.** ([#669](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/669))
- **A draw under `Context2D::set_filter_css` casts the shadow of what the filter makes, where it cast the shadow of the draw unfiltered.** ([#713](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/713))
- **A draw casting a shadow lands the pixels it lands casting none, where a draw under `blur()` moved by up to 15 levels and one under `drop-shadow()` further.** ([#714](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/714))
