### Fixed

- **A run of text casting a shadow under a `globalCompositeOperation` other than `source-over` composites its shadow and then the run as one image, as the HTML Standard paints it.** ([#807](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/807), [#740](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/740))
- **A shadowed rectangle, path or image under `source-in`, `source-out`, `destination-in`, `destination-out`, `destination-atop` or `copy` composites its shadow and then itself, each over the whole canvas.** ([#807](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/807), [#786](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/786))
- **`drawParagraph` under a `globalAlpha` or a `globalCompositeOperation` composites the paragraph's backgrounds, each text shadow and its text one after another, as `fillRect` and `fillText` do.** ([#807](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/807), [#788](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/788))
- **A run of text holding colour glyphs casts one shadow from the whole run, as a run of plain glyphs does, not one for each colour glyph and one for the plain glyphs between them.** ([#807](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/807), [#787](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/787))
- **A canvas drawn into another draws its text with the glyph edges its own pixels have, under its own `textContrast` and `textGamma` rather than Skia's defaults.** ([#807](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/807), [#805](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/805))
