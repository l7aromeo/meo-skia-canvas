### Fixed

- **`drawCanvas()` composites the source canvas as its own pixels: transparent pixels put there with `putImageData()` leave the destination showing rather than erasing it, and its overlapping translucent draws composite with one another first.** ([#829](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/829), [#822](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/822))
- **Curve edges of a canvas drawn with `drawCanvas()` at a whole-pixel offset match the source's own pixels, as `drawImage()` draws them, however a clip, a crop or the canvas's edge cuts it.** ([#829](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/829), [#822](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/822))
- **A PDF or SVG of a canvas holding `putImageData()` shows what the canvas does: in an SVG the put replaces what lay beneath it, and a PDF of a canvas drawn in with `drawCanvas()` that holds one no longer erases the page beneath it.** ([#829](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/829), [#822](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/822))
