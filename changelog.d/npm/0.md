### Fixed

- **An SVG image's `href` names what `xlink:href` does -- an `<image>`, a `<use>` target, a gradient or pattern to inherit from -- and wins over an `xlink:href` beside it.** ([#0](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/0), [#734](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/734))
- **On Linux, `measureText` and every drawn run advance each glyph by its font units times the size, as macOS and Windows do, rather than about 2/65536 px an em over at 16px in a 1000-unit face.** ([#0](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/0), [#695](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/695))
- **An SVG document's paint in any CSS Color 4 form -- `color()`, `oklch()`, `#rrggbbaa`, space-separated `rgb()`, `transparent` -- is the colour it names, as a `fillStyle` reads it, and a `currentcolor` in lower case is the `color` it inherits, where both drew black.** ([#0](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/0), [#815](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/815))
