### Fixed

- **A `shadowBlur` shadow is the exact Gaussian blur of what casts it, on the CPU and the GPU, where Skia's approximation was up to 11 levels off it.** ([#669](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/669))
- **A draw under `filter` casts the shadow of what the filter makes, as Chrome does, where it cast the shadow of the draw unfiltered.** ([#713](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/713))
- **A draw casting a shadow lands the pixels it lands casting none, where a draw under `blur()` moved by up to 15 levels and one under `drop-shadow()` further.** ([#714](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/714))
- **An image or a canvas drawn under a `filter` of several colour functions, and text under `blur()` and them, takes the tint the functions make together, where rounding between them moved an opaque pixel by up to 4 levels.** ([#730](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/730))
- **A fill or stroke in a pattern or a gradient under `blur()` is the exact Gaussian blur of what it draws, where Skia's blur was up to 5.78 levels off it.** ([#715](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/715))
- **A rectangle under `blur()` on a GPU canvas is its exact blur to the byte, as on a CPU canvas, where the GPU was up to 0.86 of a level off it.** ([#708](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/708))
