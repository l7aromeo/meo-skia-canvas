### Fixed

- **Translucent text on a CPU canvas lands the exact product of its coverage and its alpha, where about one covered pixel in ten stood a level off it.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#761](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/761))
- **Translucent text on a GPU canvas keeps the glyph contrast of the colour it is drawn in, where a grey such as `rgba(128, 128, 128, 0.3)` landed up to 8 levels below the exact product.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#778](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/778))
- **A gradient a GPU canvas draws on the CPU -- a translucent two-point conical one, one interpolated in a space other than the canvas's, one cast with a shadow or drawn through a blend -- lands the same bytes inside a `saveLayer()` as on the canvas itself.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#772](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/772))
- **`getImageData()` of part of a canvas stores the bytes a read of the whole canvas stores there, for a path's anti-aliased edge, a gradient, a pattern, or an image off whole pixels or scaled.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#771](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/771))
- **On an x86-64 CPU, a canvas drawn with `drawCanvas()` under `destination-atop`, `source-in`, `copy` or another blend that changes the page beyond it lands its own pixels at a whole-pixel offset, as `drawImage()` of them does, where translucent pixels stood a level off.** ([#806](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/806), [#822](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/822))
