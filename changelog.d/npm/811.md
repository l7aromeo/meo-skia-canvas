### Fixed

- **A gradient with `colorInterpolationSpace = "prophoto-rgb"` mixes by CSS Color 4's curve, linear below 16/512, where it drew dark ramps up to 4 levels off; an SVG export draws it as an image, since SVG's own gradient mixes in sRGB.** ([#811](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/811), [#753](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/753))
- **On Windows, a process that loads the addon only in worker threads no longer crashes some time after the last of them ends: the addon stays loaded for the life of the process.** ([#811](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/811), [#735](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/735))
- **A full-page `fillRect` or `fill` under a transform that shrinks it, and a full-page `clearRect` under a clip, inside a layer or turned by the transform, no longer erase what they do not reach.** ([#811](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/811), [#808](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/808))
- **An opaque `drawImage` or a `putImageData` covering the whole page releases what was drawn before it, as a full-page opaque fill or `clearRect` does, so a canvas cleared that way each frame no longer grows; a background drawn in pieces still keeps what it covers, as [docs/performance.md](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/blob/main/docs/performance.md) explains.** ([#811](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/pull/811), [#741](https://github.com/l7aromeo/meo-skia-canvas/issues/741))
