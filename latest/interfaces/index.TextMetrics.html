    Interface TextMetrics

    The dimensions of a piece of text in the canvas, as created by the CanvasRenderingContext2D.measureText() method.

    The values are getters on TextMetrics.prototype, as HTML's interface defines them, and the object has no properties of its own: Object.keys is empty and a spread copies nothing. JSON.stringify gives every value, through TextMetrics.toJSON. The four actualBoundingBox values and lines come from the outline of every glyph, worked out when the first of them is read and kept for the rest.
